Skyreader is independent software: no ads, no growth team, no investors to satisfy. The free plan is meant to be genuinely usable, and the Supporter plan exists for people who read widely and want to keep Skyreader running.

## Plans

|                                   | Free  | Supporter |
| --------------------------------- | ----- | --------- |
| Active feeds                      | 100   | 1,000     |
| URL saves per month               | 100   | 1,000     |
| Mirrored subscriptions            | 1,000 | 5,000     |
| Newsletters by email              | —     | ✓         |
| Search everything read            | —     | ✓         |
| PDFs and EPUBs, by link or upload | —     | ✓         |
| Pictures kept with saved articles | —     | ✓         |

Mirrored subscriptions are the headroom for what Atmospheric sync brings along from your PDS.

Supporters also get a private address for [email newsletters](/guide/adding-sources/#email-newsletters), [search](/guide/search/) across everything they've read, saved and highlighted, [PDFs](/guide/saving-and-highlights/#saving-a-pdf) and [EPUBs](/guide/saving-and-highlights/#saving-an-epub): saved by link or uploaded, read and highlighted like any article. Their saved articles also keep their [pictures](/guide/saving-and-highlights/#what-a-save-keeps), so those outlast the site. Files and pictures share one storage allowance.

Everything else, the reader, highlights, the daily magazine, the linkblog, offline support, is the same on every plan.

Current prices are on the [Supporter page](https://skyreader.app/supporter), billed monthly or yearly. While Skyreader is young, plans are at founding prices, and the price you join at is yours for as long as you stay subscribed. There's also a **Believer** tier, priced per year, for people who want to fund the future of reading on the Atmosphere; it includes everything in Supporter plus early access to new features.

## Billing

Checkout and billing run through [Polar](https://polar.sh). **Manage billing** on the Supporter page opens your billing portal, where you can change plans or cancel anytime. Cancelling never deletes anything; you keep your data and drop back to free-plan limits.

Questions about billing: [support@skyreader.app](mailto:support@skyreader.app).
