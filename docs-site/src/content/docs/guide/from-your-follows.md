---
title: From your follows
description: Read the links the people you follow on Bluesky are sharing, without the timeline.
---

**From your follows** gathers the links people you follow on Bluesky are sharing and lists them one article at a time. The article most of them shared comes first. Nothing is ranked by engagement, and the list ends.

It's in the sidebar, and the week's most-shared links also show up on **Home** as **Shared by people you follow**.

## Turning it on

The first time you open it, Skyreader asks to read your Bluesky Following timeline. Choose **Allow access**, confirm on your account provider's page, and you land back on the page, still signed in. You only do this once.

Skyreader never posts anything with it. It reads the same timeline you see in Bluesky, so anyone you've muted or blocked stays out.

## What counts as a share

- A post or quote post with a link in it.
- A repost of one. The person who reposted it counts as the sharer.
- A quote of someone else's link post. It counts as a share by the person quoting, and their words go with it.

Replies aren't included, and neither are links back to Bluesky itself.

## Reading

Open a link to read it in the reader, like any article. It isn't saved unless you save it. A link you've opened stays in the list, a little quieter.

Under each link, **… shared this** opens what each person said when they shared it, with a link to their post on Bluesky.

**Day**, **3 days**, and **Week** change how far back the list looks. The **×** on a row hides that link, on every device.

## What's kept

Skyreader keeps the links it finds for a week, then deletes them. They're stored privately on Skyreader's servers and only you can see them. Which links you opened or hid is kept a little longer, so a hidden link stays hidden.

A link that can't be read in Skyreader opens in a new tab instead.
