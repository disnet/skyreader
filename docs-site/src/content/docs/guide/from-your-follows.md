---
title: From your follows
description: Read the links the people you follow on Bluesky are sharing, without the timeline.
---

**From your follows** gathers the links people you follow on Bluesky are sharing and brings them into your reading, one article at a time. Nothing is ranked by engagement, and the list ends.

It's a source, like a feed you subscribe to. Its links arrive in a channel called **From your follows**, under **Feeds** in the sidebar, and the week's most-shared links also show up on **Home** as **Shared by people you follow**.

## Turning it on

On a new account, **Home** asks first. Under **See what people you follow are sharing**, choose **Allow access**, confirm on your account provider's page, and you come back to Home with the week's links ready to read.

The first time you open **Everything**, a box at the top asks whether to add your follows' links there. **Add them to Everything** turns it on; **Not now** leaves Everything as it is. You're only asked once.

You can also go to **Manage Sources** and find **Links from people you follow**. Skyreader asks to read your Bluesky Following timeline. Choose **Allow**, confirm on your account provider's page, and you come back still signed in. You only do this once, and it makes the channel for you.

Skyreader never posts anything with it. It reads the same timeline you see in Bluesky, so anyone you've muted or blocked stays out.

## What counts as a share

- A post or quote post with a link in it.
- A repost of one. The person who reposted it counts as the sharer.
- A quote of someone else's link post. It counts as a share by the person quoting, and their words go with it.

Replies aren't included, and neither are links back to Bluesky itself.

## Reading

Each link reads like any article in the channel. It's dated by when it was first shared, and **Shared by** says who shared it. Open it to read it in the reader. It isn't saved unless you save it. Mark it read, or open it, and it leaves the unread list like everything else.

When people you follow share an article that's already in your feeds, you see it once: the article, with **Shared by** on it.

Open any article, from a feed, your saves, or anywhere else, and if people you follow shared it, they lead its **Discussion**, marked **You follow**, with what they said. **People you follow** narrows the discussion to just them.

## Everything and other channels

Your follows' links show in **Everything** only if you said yes to that. Change it any time with **Show them in Everything too** on **Manage Sources**. They never add to your unread counts.

The **From your follows** channel is an ordinary channel. Rename it, change its filters, or delete it. To add your follows' links to another channel, edit that channel's sources, choose **Include only**, and check **People you follow on Bluesky**.

## What's kept

Skyreader keeps the links it finds for a week, then deletes them. They're stored privately on Skyreader's servers and only you can see them. Which ones you've read is kept with the rest of your read state.

A link that can't be read in Skyreader opens in a new tab instead.
