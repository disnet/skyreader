title: Bluesky feeds
description: Read your Bluesky timeline, or any feed you saved in Bluesky, as a channel, and like, repost and reply from it.
---

Your Bluesky **Following** timeline, and the feeds you pinned or saved in Bluesky, can be sources in Skyreader. Each one becomes a channel of posts you read alongside everything else, with the few things you'd do to a post: reply, repost and like.

It's a quiet client, not a second Bluesky. Posts read in their own channel, a link in a post opens in the reader, and nothing counts toward your unread badges.

## Adding a feed

Go to **Manage Sources** and find **Bluesky feeds**. It lists **Following on Bluesky** and every feed you saved in Bluesky, in the order you pinned them there.

The first time, choose **Allow access** and confirm on your account provider's page. Skyreader asks to read your Bluesky feeds; reading never posts anything. If you already turned on [From your follows](/guide/from-your-follows/), your Following timeline works without asking again.

Choose **Add** next to a feed. It gets its own channel under **Feeds** in the sidebar, named after the feed. **Open** takes you there.

## Reading

A feed on its own keeps Bluesky's order: the Following timeline is newest first, and a custom feed is ranked however its maker ranks it. Scroll to the end and the next page loads.

A post with a link shows its link card. Choose the card, or **Read in Skyreader**, to read the page in the reader. It isn't saved unless you save it; the bookmark on a post saves the page it links to. **Open on Bluesky** opens the post itself.

Media that Bluesky labels as sensitive stays hidden until you choose **Show**.

## Liking, reposting and replying

The first time you like, repost or reply, Skyreader asks for one more permission: to post to Bluesky as you. Choose **Allow** and confirm with your account provider. Skyreader only posts when you do.

- **Like** and **Repost** take effect right away. Choose them again to undo.
- **Reply** opens a box under the post. Replies are up to 300 characters, and links, @mentions and #tags work the way they do in Bluesky. Press **Reply**, or Cmd+Enter (Ctrl+Enter on Windows), to post.

With a post selected, **l** likes it.

## Channels and Everything

Bluesky feeds never show in **Everything**, and a busy timeline can't flood the rest of your reading. A feed shows only in a channel that names it.

The feed's channel is an ordinary channel. Rename it or delete it. To mix a feed into another channel, edit that channel's sources, choose **Include only**, and check the feed. Mixed with other sources, posts sort by time.

To stop reading a feed, remove it on **Manage Sources**. Its channel goes with it.

## What's kept

Skyreader keeps which feeds you added, privately on its servers. It doesn't keep the posts: each channel reads the feed from Bluesky when you open it. Likes, reposts and replies are records in your own Bluesky account, public like anything else you post there.
