## Feeds

**Feeds** shows everything you follow in one chronological stream. The sidebar lists your sources and channels; **channels** are saved views over your feeds, filtered by source or read state, so "Newsletters" or "Slow reads" is one click away.

The **Sources** filter lists everything that brings you things to read, grouped:

- **From Bluesky**: **Your likes** and **Your bookmarks** (the links in posts you liked or bookmarked, if you've turned them on), and **People you follow** (the links they share).
- **Newsletters**: every sender, together or one by one.
- **Publications**: your feeds, by folder.

Everything starts checked. Uncheck a source, a folder or a whole group to leave it out. Choose **only** beside one to see just that. **Show everything** puts it all back. A group or folder stays whole as it grows: hide **Newsletters** and a newsletter that arrives tomorrow stays hidden too. On **Feeds**, the filter and the unread/all choice stay put when you come back or reload, on that device. Choose **Save** (on a phone, **Save as channel**) to keep a filter as a channel.

### Making a channel

Choose **New channel** under **Feeds** or **Library**, in the sidebar or the feed switcher on a phone.

- **Name** fills itself in from what you choose. Type your own to keep it.
- **Show** and **Order** set what the channel opens on: unread or everything, newest or oldest first.
- **What's in it**, for a channel of your feeds:
  - **Sources I pick**: check the sources you want. Check a folder to take in everything filed there, now and later.
  - **Sources that match**: a rule picks them and keeps up as you follow more. **Daily Digest** takes sources that post several times a day, **Don't Miss** the ones that post rarely, and **Long Reads** the ones that write at length. You can also match the sources you added lately, ones you tagged, or ones from a site.
- **What's in it**, for a channel of saved articles: narrow by kind, when you saved it, length, site or tag. Leave a row empty to take in all of it.

To change a channel later, open it and choose **Edit channel**, or right-click it in the sidebar. On a phone, **Edit channel** sits beside **View** in the view options.

![The Feeds view: sources and channels in the sidebar, newest articles first in the stream](../../../assets/screenshots/library.png)

Items you scroll past can be marked read automatically (a toggle in **Settings → Reading**).

**Home** is the calmer front page: pick up where you left off, recent saves, a few things from your Shelf, your daily magazine. It draws on your [Library](/guide/saving-and-highlights/#your-library-queue-and-shelf), and each lane links to where it comes from. **Continue reading** follows the same rule as the Shelf's: anything you've started and not finished, including a book you archived halfway through that's still on your Shelf.

To change Home, choose **Customize** at the top of it. Pick where the app opens and how large
the cards are, hide any section, or drag one by its handle to reorder. Changes apply right away
and are saved on this device.

## The reader

Opening an article gives you a clean, full-screen reading surface.

Skyreader preserves the structure of Leaflet and Offprint publications, including galleries,
equations, buttons, task lists, and aligned text. If a publication uses an interactive widget the
reader cannot safely show, the article says so and links you to the original instead of silently
leaving content out. Members-only sections stay on their publication.

![The reader: a full-screen article with quiet chrome and a Discussion section at the end](../../../assets/screenshots/reader.png)

- **Style customization** : change the font style and size.
- **Paged mode**: a toggle in the reader switches from scrolling to page turns, one page at a time, or two columns side by side on a wide screen.
- **Contents**: an article with sections, a PDF or a book lists them under **Contents**: beside **Back** on a wide screen, in the bar at the bottom on a phone, or press **c**. The section you're in is marked; choose another to go there. A PDF or a book lists its own table of contents where it has one, in either view of a PDF.
- **Light and dark**: Skyreader follows your device's setting. To keep it light or dark regardless, pick a theme in **Settings → Reading → Appearance**.
- **Highlighting**: double-click a paragraph or select text. See [Saving and highlights](/guide/saving-and-highlights/).
- **Images**: click an image to see it full screen. Click again or press **Esc** to close it.

![Paged mode: the same article flowed into two side-by-side columns, turned a page at a time](../../../assets/screenshots/paged-reader.png)

## Discussion

Articles carry a **Discussion** section at the end that gathers what people across the Atmosphere have written about that link: linkblog notes, Leaflet comments, Bluesky posts, Margin annotations, Semble connections, merged into one stream.

To add yours, use the buttons under **Add yours**. **Bluesky** writes a post from your own account, with the article's link card under your words; it's public like any Bluesky post.

## The daily magazine

**Daily** builds a magazine issue from your saved articles. Pick an issue length in minutes and an ordering, then **Generate issue**. An issue is a snapshot: new saves don't reshuffle it, and it resumes where you left off on any device. When you finish, archive the issue or generate a new one from the current pile.

![A daily magazine issue: a dated table of contents built from saved articles, followed by the first article](../../../assets/screenshots/daily-magazine.png)

## Offline

Your feeds, saves, and highlights are stored on your device. Reading, marking read, highlighting, and the magazine all work offline; anything you do is queued and synced when you reconnect. Adding sources and changing sync settings need a connection.
