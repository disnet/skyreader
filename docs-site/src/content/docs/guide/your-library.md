title: Your Library
description: Where your saves live. Read through them in the Queue, keep what lasts on the Shelf, and group it into collections.

Your **Library** is everything you've saved. It's one set of saves, seen two ways:

- The **Queue** is what to read next, newest first. Read something, choose **Archive**, and it moves from the inbox to the archive.
- The **Shelf** is what you keep and come back to, like books, papers, and references. Finishing something doesn't take it off.

**Library** in the sidebar opens the Queue. Switch with **Queue** and **Shelf** at the top of either.

## The Queue and the Shelf together

Because they're the same saves, an item can be in both, and what you do in one doesn't change the other:

- **Archiving** clears an item from the Queue's inbox. If it's on your Shelf, it stays there, marked **Archived**.
- In the Queue, an item that's also on your Shelf is marked **On Shelf**.
- Each item's **⋯** menu shows both side by side: **Archive** under Queue, **Add to Shelf** under Shelf.

## Putting things on the Shelf

Books and PDFs go on the Shelf as soon as you save them. To put anything else there, choose **Add to Shelf** from its **⋯** menu, or from the **⋯** menu while you're reading it. **Take off Shelf** removes it, and the save stays in your Queue.

The Shelf opens on **Continue reading**, the things you're partway through. Below that is everything on it, as covers or as a list. Filter by title or author, show only books, PDFs, or articles, and sort by what you read last, what was most recently connected, what you saved last, title, or author.

## Collections

Collections group what's on your Shelf, like a course's readings or a project's sources.

A row at the top of the Shelf holds the ones you've pinned and the ones you opened last; **All** lists every collection, with a search. To keep a collection on the row, pin it from that list.

Create one with **New collection** at the bottom of the list, then add items with **Add to collection**, from their **⋯** menu in the Queue or on the Shelf, or from the reader's **⋯** menu. An item can be in more than one collection. A collection keeps its own order (**Move up** and **Move down** in the item menu) and can carry a short description.

Your collections are also listed under **Library** in the sidebar and the feed switcher, so you can open one from anywhere. To change their order, drag one up or down the sidebar. On a phone, press and hold one and choose **Move up** or **Move down**.

### Working on many at once

To file many items at once, choose **Select** above the Queue or the Shelf, tap the items you want (or **Select all**), then **Add to collection**. In a collection, **Remove** takes the selected items out of it. In the Queue's Inbox, **Archive** archives the selected items. **Delete** deletes the selected items from your Library, after you confirm. A collection holds up to 2,000 items.

Collections are private to Skyreader unless you link one, and need an account and a connection.

### Linking a collection to Semble or Margin

**A linked collection is public**, because Semble and Margin collections are. Uploaded PDFs and EPUBs stay private and aren't copied over, and your highlights and notes stay in Skyreader. Before you link, Skyreader tells you how many articles go across and how many uploads stay behind, and a linked collection keeps saying how many uploads stay. A collection of only uploads can still be linked; it has nothing to add there until you add an article. The collection's order is kept in Skyreader. **Unlink** stops the syncing; both collections keep what they hold.

A collection can stay in step with one of your [Semble](https://semble.so) or [Margin](https://margin.at) collections. Open it on the Shelf and choose **Link to Semble or Margin**, then pick one of your collections there, or let Skyreader make a new one with the same name. From then on, adding or removing an item in either place does the same in the other. Something added in Semble or Margin becomes a save in your Library and joins the collection. It goes straight to the Shelf, filed in your Queue's Archive rather than its inbox; an article you'd already saved stays where it was. Changes made there come in when you open the collection.

To bring in a collection you already keep in Semble or Margin, open **All** on the Shelf, choose **Import from Semble or Margin**, and pick it. It comes in as a new collection with the same name, already linked; its items show up over the next few moments (the collection says **Syncing…** meanwhile) and then stay in step. Deleting a linked collection here leaves the Semble or Margin one as it is.

Any linked collection can also be read as a [reading room](/guide/reading-rooms/): choose **Open as room** on it to see who's reading along, and share that page's link to invite people.

## New connections from Semble

When someone on [Semble](https://semble.so) connects something to an article you've saved, the save shows a small **new** mark in the Queue and on the Shelf. It works for anything in your Library, whether or not you use Semble yourself.

The Shelf gathers these in a **New connections** lane: each item shows who connected what to it, how (supports, opposes, related, and so on), and their notes. The same lane shows on **Home**, where **Customize** can hide or move it. Tap a connection to open it in a new tab, save it, or connect back with a connection of your own, or tap the article's title to keep reading where you left off.

The article's discussion also starts on **Connections**, with the new ones marked. They stay marked until you mark them seen: with the check on the item, **Mark as seen** in the discussion, or **Mark all seen** on the lane. Your own connections never count as new.
